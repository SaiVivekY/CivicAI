Our Mission

Bridging the Legal Literacy Gap for Every Citizen

In India, millions of citizens face routine civic grievances—unrefunded security deposits, unanswered RTI queries, fraudulent billing, or municipal apathy—without knowing their legal rights or procedural remedies.

CivicAI was created as a civic-tech initiative to empower citizens with immediate, clear, and actionable intelligence. By parsing official statutes and grievance redressal mechanisms, we translate intimidating administrative bureaucracy into straightforward checklists and actionable documents.
